📍 Togo Explorer

Discover. Explore. Experience.

1. Site Name

Togo Explorer

Slogan: Discover. Explore. Experience.

Togo Explorer is a tourism website designed to help visitors discover and explore tourist attractions in Togo. The name was chosen because it clearly communicates the main purpose of the website: helping tourists explore the country's natural, cultural, historical, and recreational destinations.

The name is short, easy to remember, and directly connected to the purpose of the tourism business.

Logo

📍 Togo Explorer

The logo will use the name Togo Explorer with a simple visual element representing travel and exploration, such as a location pin, a path, or a natural landscape.

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of Togo Explorer is to provide tourists with useful information about tourist attractions and destinations in Togo.

The website will introduce visitors to different places they can discover, including natural attractions, cultural sites, historical locations, and other destinations of interest. Each destination will include relevant information to help visitors understand what they can see and experience there.

The website will also provide an interactive way for visitors to explore destinations based on their interests and begin planning a trip to Togo.

The website will serve as an online presence for a fictional tourism company that helps tourists discover Togo and choose destinations for their trips.

3. Scenarios

The following scenarios represent questions that visitors from the target audience may ask when using the website:

  • What are the best tourist attractions to visit in Togo?
  • Which destinations in Togo are best for nature, culture, or historical experiences?
  • What activities can I do at the different tourist destinations in Togo?
  • How can I choose destinations that match my interests when planning my trip to Togo?

4. Color Schema

The main color of the website will be green, representing nature, exploration, growth, and the natural beauty of Togo. The color palette will be designed with accessibility and sufficient color contrast in mind.

Forest Green

Hex: #1B5E20

Used for main headings, navigation elements, important buttons, accents, and the website logo.

Light Green

Hex: #A5D6A7

Used for secondary backgrounds, cards, decorative sections, and subtle accents.

Off White

Hex: #F8F9F5

Used as the main page background to keep the website clean and easy to read.

Dark Charcoal

Hex: #1F2933

Used for body text, paragraphs, and general information.

The main combination will be forest green with an off-white background and dark charcoal text. Green accents will be used consistently throughout the website to create a recognizable visual identity without making the pages difficult to read.

5. Typography

The website will use two fonts to create a clear and professional visual hierarchy.

Ubuntu, sans-serif — Heading Font

Togo Explorer — Discover. Explore. Experience.

Poppins will be used for main headings, section headings, navigation elements, buttons, and the website name. Poppins provides a modern and friendly appearance that fits a tourism website.

Barlow, sans-serif — Body Font

Discover the beauty, culture, history, and natural attractions of Togo.

Open Sans will be used for paragraphs, descriptions, destination information, form labels, and general website content. Open Sans is highly readable and will make longer sections of information easier to read.

Typography Hierarchy

The website will use larger and heavier text for headings and smaller, readable text for paragraphs. Consistent font sizes, spacing, and line heights will be used throughout the site to improve readability and usability.
